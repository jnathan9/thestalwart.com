How the data is organized

Every contribution gets a permanent list ID and nine item memberships. Each membership has an item ID and a position in the original image. That preserves the relationships within and between books and albums, not just a tally of popular titles. Book and album lists from the same person share a public contributor ID.

Use the data for recommendations, co-occurrence networks, research, or your own projects. Names and post links are optional; attribution is self-reported.
