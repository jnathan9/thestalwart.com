Meet Your Neighbors

Everybody understands that traditional political categories like Left and Right mean nothing anymore. So people are coming up with increasingly esoteric categories like "post-populist socialist," "Dengist techno-determinist," or "Agrarian-constitutional liberal nationalist." These don't work either.

Meet Your Neighbors is a new kind of political compass. The goal isn't to tell you what category you belong to but rather to simply identify who talks and thinks like you. In other words, the goal is to identify your neighbors without naming your neighborhood.

After a user uploads their X archives, a model then reads through them and produces a series of statements summarizing their worldview and interests. These short summaries are then run through an embedding model, plotting the user on a series of coordinates. The map reveals where they land, and who is nearby. Some names on the site come from tweets that users have already uploaded to Community-Archive.org.
